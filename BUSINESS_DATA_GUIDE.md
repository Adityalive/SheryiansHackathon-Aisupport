# 📋 Business Data Guide — AI FAQ Generation

> The more context your business provides, the better the AI can answer customer questions automatically.
> If a customer asks something the AI has no data on, it will **automatically create a support ticket** for your team to handle manually.

---

## Why This Matters

The AI generates FAQs and answers customer queries based **only on the data your business has provided**.

| Situation | AI Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Business has return policy data | ✅ AI answers return questions instantly |
| Business has NOT added return policy | ⚠️ AI creates a ticket for your team |
| Business has shipping info | ✅ AI answers delivery time questions |
| Business has NOT added shipping info | ⚠️ AI creates a ticket for your team |

The goal is to **minimise tickets** by giving the AI everything it needs upfront.

## ✅ Section 9: Support Escalation Rules (For Your Team)

> Define when the AI should **stop trying** and raise a ticket automatically.

| Scenario | Recommended Action |
|---|---|
| Customer reports damaged/wrong item | 🎫 Always create a ticket |
| Legal complaints or fraud reports | 🎫 Always create a ticket |
| Refund not received after 10+ days | 🎫 Always create a ticket |
| Question about a specific order not in system | 🎫 Always create a ticket |
| Complaint about a delivery partner | 🎫 Always create a ticket |
| AI confidence is low on any answer | 🎫 Create a ticket with AI's best attempt as a note |
